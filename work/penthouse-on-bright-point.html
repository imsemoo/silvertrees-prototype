home / work / Penthouse on Bright Point

penthouse on bright point.

A holiday apartment on Magnetic Island that lets the views do the selling.

the brief

Showcasing a beautiful holiday apartment on Magnetic Island, this website truly allows the images to speak more than a thousand words…

Background images are placed behind the page content on several pages, to give visitors a panoramic view of the surrounding area, whilst allowing interior shots and test to sit on top. The background images are simple to upload and change via the site administration area, where the system ensures each image will cover the full width of the browser page (regardless of screen resolution).

View the property and book online with the dynamic booking system built into the site. Visitors can check availability, view price information for selected dates and submit their booking without having to leave the site.

Take a virtual tour of the property by viewing the video and slideshow gallery on the site. Both these functions are fully manageable in the admin area; upload a new video, edit and/or add more images to the gallery and remove any files as required. The built-in site administration is simple to use and requires no technical expertise, allowing the site owner to make changes to text and image content as often as they like.
