online curtains.

Made-to-measure curtains, configured step by step and priced on the spot.

the brief

For custom curtains, this site offers a simple step by step process, so customers can easily create the perfect curtain.

There is also the warehouse for special bargains…

The site offers a simple process for the customer, but comprehensive admin functionality to manage all aspects of the site – from quotes to orders, editable parameter based calculation across all custom styles, workroom and supplier orders, customer management, newsletters, news and events…
