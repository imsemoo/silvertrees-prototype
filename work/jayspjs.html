jayspjs.

Pyjamas sold by size, with stock control the owner manages in-house.

the brief

JaysPJs website was designed to enhance buyer experience through attractive product displays, accessible product information and size functionality, which enables customers to add the products they wish to buy to their shopping basket straight away.

The website is easily maintained by the site owner, who has the ability to enter various sizes for any product item, manage and upload product images, edit site content and of course manage orders. The linked database includes Stock Control, which makes the shopping cart functionality simple and intuitive to maintain.
