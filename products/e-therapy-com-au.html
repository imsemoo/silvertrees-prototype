e-therapy

Secure online therapy sessions, booked with the therapist you choose.

the brief

e-therapy offers you the latest in secure online therapy sessions. All you need to do is select a therapist & register as a client. Once you have completed registration you will be able to book your first online session, it's that easy!

The e-therapy system offers flexibility to both clients and therapists. e-therapy allows clients to book secure chat sessions online, selecting from a range of different therapists.

All therapists who register with e-therapy receive their own sub-domain and are easy to locate on our therapist search page. The e-therapy service is available – and useful – for all types of therapists: psychologists, nutritionists, counsellors, occupational therapists ... and many more.
